About the Department

The Department of Computer Science is committed to excellence in teaching, research, and innovation. Our faculty members are actively engaged in cutting-edge research areas such as Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Data Science, and Web Technologies. We aim to provide students with a strong foundation in computer science principles while encouraging creativity, problem-solving, and collaboration. The department also organizes workshops, seminars, and hackathons to ensure students gain practical exposure and industry-ready skills.
